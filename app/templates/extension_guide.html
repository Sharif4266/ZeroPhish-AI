Install ZeroPhish AI

Protect your browser with real-time neural threat analysis.

1

Open Extensions Manager

Open your Chrome browser and navigate to the extensions page by typing this in your address bar:

chrome://extensions
2

Enable Developer Mode

In the top right corner of the extensions page, toggle the Developer mode switch to ON.

3

Load Extension Folder

Click the Load unpacked button and select the extension folder located in your ZeroPhish AI project directory.
