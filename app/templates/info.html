ZeroPhish is an advanced security platform built to defend users against the evolving landscape of digital threats. Our focus is on providing high-accuracy phishing detection through neural analysis and real-time heuristics.

Our Mission

We aim to make the digital world safer by democratizing high-end security tools. Phishing remains the #1 entry point for cyberattacks, and ZeroPhish provides the first line of defense for both individuals and enterprises.

Core Principles

  • Data Minimization: we only process what is necessary to determine risk.

  • User Autonomy: providing clear, actionable insights without hidden agendas.

Information Processing

ZeroPhish processes URLs and message content provided by users. This data is analyzed in volatile memory and is not persisted unless specifically requested for deep audit purposes.

No Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your information to outside parties. Our revenue model is based on premium API access, not data monetization.

Security Measures

  • End-to-end TLS encryption for all data in transit.
  • Regular security audits of our detection infrastructure.
  • Strict internal access controls to scanning metadata.
